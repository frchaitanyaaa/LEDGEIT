# ADR-006: Dataset — NEU-DET

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-14

NEU surface defect database: 1,800 grayscale 200×200 images of hot-rolled steel, 6 classes (crazing, inclusion, patches, pitted_surface, rolled-in_scale, scratches) with bounding boxes. Ties directly to the Kobe Steel case study. Images are git-ignored; `datasets/README.md` explains the download and conversion to YOLO format.
